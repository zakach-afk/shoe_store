How To Best Care for Your SOLO Footwear Shoes

  • Use shoe wax or high-quality leather cream regularly.
  • Keep your shoes away from water, dust, and direct sunlight.
  • Store your shoes in the SOLO Footwear shoebox or cloth dust bags when not in use.

Preserving the Elegance of Your SOLO Footwear Shoes

Proper shoe care maintains their elegance. Remove your shoes carefully by hand at the end of the day. Give them a break by rotating pairs, and use shoe trees for storage.

Preventing Wear and Tear for Long-Lasting Shoes

Neglecting shoe care can lead to quicker deterioration. Dirt and debris can get stuck in the soles, leading to expansion and eventual cracking. Regular cleaning prevents this and ensures your shoes last longer.

Protecting Your Feet and Avoiding Odors

Shoe care not only keeps your footwear in top condition but also helps prevent foot infections and odors. Our shoes and medicated insoles are treated to stay bacteria-free, but cleaning and airing them out keeps your feet healthy and fresh.

Shoe Care Habits for Longevity

Taking good care of your shoes ensures they look great and last longer. Always avoid washing leather shoes in a washing machine, store them with proper airflow, and avoid over-wearing a single pair to prevent structural damage.

Additional Tips for Longer-Lasting Shoes:

  • Dry your feet before wearing shoes to avoid moisture damage.
  • Wipe off mud and liquids immediately, especially on pure leather shoes.
  • Rotate your shoes to give them time to breathe between wears.
  • Use quality wooden shoe trees to absorb moisture and maintain shape.
  • Avoid wearing leather-soled shoes in excessively wet conditions. If they do get wet, stuff them with paper and allow them to dry naturally at room temperature.
