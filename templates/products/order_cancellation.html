ORDER CANCELLATION

At SOLO FOOTWEAR, we understand that circumstances may change, and you might need to cancel your order. To initiate the cancellation process, please follow these steps:

Cancellation Process:

1. Contact Us:

To cancel your order, please email us at warisali942015@gmail.com or call / WhatsApp us at 03192255100. Make sure to include the following details in your message:

  • Your Full Name
  • Order Number / Contact Number
  • Items to Cancel

2. Await Confirmation:

Our customer support team will process your cancellation request as soon as possible. You will receive a confirmation message once the cancellation is complete.

Refund Process:

Online Payments:

  • If you made an online payment, the refund will be processed to the original payment method but only if we delivered you the wrong/damaged articles or if you placed an order and we don’t have that article in our stock.
  • Allow 1-3 business days for the refund to reflect in your account, depending on your payment provider.

Cash on Delivery (COD):

For COD orders, our customer support team will guide you on the cancellation process. Please provide the necessary details during your cancellation request before the parcel is dispatched.
