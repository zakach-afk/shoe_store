FAQS

Frequently Asked Questions & Customer Support

Q. How can I place the order?

You can place an order directly on our website or you can call us on 03192255100 to place an order over the phone.

Q. How long will it take for my order to be delivered?

All our orders placed before 4:00 PM (Monday–Saturday) are dispatched within 1 working day. However, due to non-availability of stock for some items it may take up to 2 - 3 working days for the order to dispatch. All orders will be delivered within 2-3 working days after placing the order.

Q. How will my parcel be delivered?

The parcel will be delivered by TCS, Call Courier, or Post-Ex Courier Services. Deliveries are made between 08:00 AM and 05:00 PM.

Q. Can I have my shoes delivered to an address other than my home address?

Yes, you can. Please ensure that you enter the different delivery address while placing the order at checkout.

Q. Can I track my order?

Yes you can. We will send the tracking information to your phone/email, or please call us on 03192255100 to obtain the Tracking ID of your parcel and you will be able to track your parcel online.

Q. Can I make changes to my order?

You can make any changes to your order before completing checkout. However, if you have already completed the order then please call us immediately on 03192255100 and we will make the changes for you. (Please note that changes cannot be made once the parcel has been dispatched).

Q. Can I cancel my order?

Yes you can cancel your order. Please call or WhatsApp us on 03192255100 before 1:00 PM and we will cancel the order for you. Please be informed that we will not be able to cancel the order if it has already been dispatched.

Q. What is your Refund & Exchange Policy?

We offer a 15-day hassle-free size exchange guarantee on all unworn footwear in original condition. For full information, please visit our Return & Exchange Policy.

Still have questions?

Our support team is available on WhatsApp to help with your orders and queries.
